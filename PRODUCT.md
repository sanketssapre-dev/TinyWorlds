# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors who want to watch and explore an autonomous miniature Swiss railway world.

## Product Purpose

Tiny Worlds presents compact, self-contained worlds to observe and explore. ASilentSail is a Swiss Alpine railway world in which two trains travel autonomously while visitors switch between Train Follow, Free Planet Spin, and Driver's view.

## Positioning

An explorable miniature planet where an autonomous railway journey is the center of the world.

## Operating Context

Visitors watch the scene in a browser. They can cycle between the three camera modes; Free Planet Spin supports wheel and pinch zoom. The trains and world continue moving without requiring the visitor to drive.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- ASilentSail is a standalone app within the Tiny Worlds repository and must remain independent and autonomous.
- Preserve the existing railway experience and its three camera modes while improving the Alpine world.
- The visitor's primary job is observation and exploration, not train operation.

## Brand Commitments

- The product is named Tiny Worlds.
- ASilentSail depicts a miniature Swiss Alpine railway world.
- The three user-provided Swiss railway landscape images are binding visual references for the landscape, vegetation, mountain composition, settlements, train, and track.

## Evidence on Hand

- Three Swiss Alpine railway landscape references supplied by the user in the project conversation.
- A runnable ASilentSail app with autonomous trains, camera modes, procedural terrain, lakes, forests, chalets, and railway infrastructure.
- No claim of geographic or railway simulation accuracy has been established.

## Product Principles

- The world is a complete miniature landscape, not a sparse set of props.
- The railway journey remains autonomous and immediately legible.
- Scale relationships should make terrain, settlements, vegetation, trains, and track feel like one coherent world.
- Visual ambition must not compromise the independent railway app or its existing exploration controls.
